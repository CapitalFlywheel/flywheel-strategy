# Historical execution research — not the active Solana launch route

The current owner-wallet launch does not deploy this program or bind reserve funds to a proposal. The active 40% reserve destination is a separate user-controlled wallet. This file is retained as historical research, not as an operational checklist

# Solana governance reserve executor: implementation plan

Status: engineering design plus an undeployed program prototype. The present `programs/solana-governance` source records votes, protects committed vault balance from its ordinary free-withdrawal instruction, and contains bounded paths for `LOCK_MSTRX`, `ACCUMULATE`, Pump/PumpSwap buybacks, CAPITAL burn/hold/lock, and Raydium MSTRx-to-SOL marketing sale. The new swap paths passed a locked SBF build and 42 host tests on 25 September 2026, but have no live transaction proof. `initialize`, `create_proposal`, trader funding and the swap executors remain hard-disabled, so this artifact cannot receive governed funds or execute a real ballot. Nothing in this document is a deployed guarantee

## Selected boundary

The owner confirmed binding execution: the exact reserve amount present and available when a proposal opens stays unavailable to the owner during the vote and, if a spending option wins, until that precise decision is executed. The owner retains control of later/uncommitted MSTRx reserve deposits. Funded holder rewards remain outside this program entirely

The owner selected a holder-only failure path: as soon as a finalized spending winner reaches its `executable_at` and remains unexecuted, the admin may publish a fresh snapshot and open a new ballot over the **same exact committed amount**. No failed-transaction proof is required: a reverted Solana transaction cannot record durable failure state in the governance account. An initial proposal starts voting 24 hours after its onchain creation; a replacement ballot starts voting at its onchain creation time. Each replacement ballot still lasts the selected one to twelve hours and has the five-minute result delay after closing. The old proposal is marked `SUPERSEDED` and its executor must reject it; new reserve deposits stay outside the ballot. A replacement ballot with no quorum or a tie retains the commitment and allows another replacement ballot as soon as that ballot's own `executable_at` has passed and `finalize` records its result. A winning `ACCUMULATE` releases the commitment to free reserve only after holders explicitly select it. The admin can choose when to publish a replacement ballot and controls the snapshot publication process, but cannot unilaterally spend or unlock committed funds through this instruction. This design does not guarantee liveness if the admin never creates a new ballot

Both paths reject a snapshot more than 25 minutes older than the onchain creation time. The typed offchain `auditCreateRevoteDraft` mirrors the Rust account order and rechecks the prior full commitment, the previous ballot's own execution time and two-RPC finalized account identities. It estimates the replacement ballot to start at the observed clock, close after the chosen voting duration, and become finalizable five minutes later; the actual transaction uses its onchain clock. Distinct finalized views may have different free-reserve balances from ordinary fee ingress; the draft uses the lower verified free balance and rejects any disagreement in the frozen commitment or immutable account fields. The panel and publisher now have local signed workflows for the exact proposal and holder snapshot, but their release gates remain closed pending the full reserve-action review and mainnet canary. Root/source artifact prepublication is operator-trusted, not independently enforced by onchain source-file hashes

The fee splitter must send the 40% MSTRx reserve share to the governance program's Token-2022 vault ATA, owned by a program-derived authority (PDA). Sending it to an ordinary owner-controlled wallet and obtaining a token delegate approval would not enforce this boundary: the wallet owner could revoke or bypass it. The ordinary reserve wallet in the current test configuration cannot be represented as a binding governed vault

## Accounts and invariants

- `config`: one PDA that currently pins the owner, official MSTRx mint, fixed SOL marketing recipient and one-time eventual CAPITAL mint/token-program binding. The protected offchain binder verifies the creator and creation event; that proof is not checked by the program. A completed executor must additionally pin the exact Pump/PumpSwap program IDs, venue identities and token programs onchain. Never accept a ticker as an identity
- `vault_authority`: PDA signer controlled only by fixed program instructions; its MSTRx ATA is the strategic reserve. A distinct holder-reward ATA and its authority must never be passed as a writable account to executor instructions
- `proposal`: one vote record with an exact full MSTRx commitment, fixed winner, lock term and recipient. One active or committed proposal at a time until its entire action completes or holders supersede it through a new ballot. The current prototype has no partial-execution fields, and the one-transaction execution rule does not need them. A failed execution rolls back entirely, leaving the full commitment for retry or a fresh ballot
- `free_raw_mstrx = reserve_ata.amount - active_reserved_raw_mstrx`. Creation freezes the entire **available** balance. New deposits are free and may be withdrawn by owner signature to a fixed disclosed recovery ATA, subject to `withdraw <= free`. Existing timed-lock accounts are separate and excluded until mature funds are returned
- `proposal-trader`: a **separate, data-empty, System-Program-owned PDA**, funded with SOL before any buyback. Both Pump `buy_exact_quote_in_v2` and PumpSwap `buy_exact_quote_in` require the `user` signer, and the latter explicitly charges that user for potentially missing fee/volume accounts. The existing `config` PDA owns program data and cannot be the payer for a System Program transfer (`Transfer: from must not carry data`). Its reserve ATA also cannot simply be supplied as the trader's quote ATA because Pump validates the user-owned canonical ATA. In one transaction, transfer the entire frozen amount from the `config` reserve ATA to the trader's Token-2022 ATA, invoke the verified exact-quote-in venue with the trader PDA signer, then burn or escrow all acquired CAPITAL. Account for any permissionless pre-existing deposits by recording baseline balances, and reject any spend of that baseline, any unspent committed MSTRx, wrong ATA, wrong SOL account owner/data or unexpected token-account closure; a partial fill rolls back the complete decision. The reserve asset never pays SOL operating costs. The panel must show this SOL rent buffer; any recovery instruction may return it only to the fixed admin address when no spending decision is awaiting execution, so the admin cannot intentionally starve a passed ballot of its required rent. This path still needs a disposable onchain canary with the actual token and both venue phases before production release
- `hold_authority` and `hold_ata`: custody of CAPITAL bought under `BUYBACK_HOLD`, with no withdrawal instruction or delegate. Time-lock ATAs should be proposal-specific so tranches cannot be mixed. For `BUYBACK_LOCK`, a permissionless release after the exact term sends CAPITAL to permanent hold, matching the old EVM behavior. For `LOCK_MSTRX`, a mature time-lock release returns MSTRx to the free strategic reserve. A permanent term has no release instruction
- Validate every writable token account's mint, token program, owner, ATA derivation and expected PDA seeds. Reject arbitrary target programs, destinations, mint substitutions, pool indices, fee recipients, router instruction bytes, remaining-account metas and signer privilege escalation. The pre-migration bonding curve is Pump's `['bonding-curve', CAPITAL mint]` PDA. The post-migration canonical PumpSwap pool must have index `0`, the same two mints, and the Pump-derived `['pool-authority', CAPITAL mint]` creator; derive its pool PDA from the official IDL rather than accepting a pool chosen by the keeper. Read and verify live phase state before execution
- `ACCUMULATE` and no-quorum/tie are distinct: the former is a winning no-op marked `EXECUTED` by `finalize` after the five-minute delay, while the latter are rejected outcomes. Both release the exact commitment into free reserve without moving tokens. A spending winner stays reserved even after voting closes and while external execution is failing. The owner explicitly selected the `ACCUMULATE` release rule
- A re-vote is distinct from an ordinary proposal: its no-quorum/tie status is 7/8 and does **not** release the carried commitment. The prototype's status 5 marks the prior decision superseded and status 6 marks the new open re-vote. The `ProposalRevoteCreated` event links old/new IDs, with no account-layout change or direct on-account parent field

An unrestricted owner-held program upgrade authority defeats any claimed freeze. The owner selected permanent removal of the governance program's upgrade authority after independent review. `CreateProposal` now requires the loader-v3 ProgramData associated with this exact program and rejects it while any upgrade authority remains. The release order is strict: review and build a binary with every required release gate `true`, deploy/upgrade and verify its actual program ID and bytes, then revoke and verify removal of upgrade authority, and only then create a binding proposal. **Do not revoke while the deployed binary retains a required `false` gate**: that would make governance permanently unavailable under that program ID. An ordinary owner recovery method may never withdraw active commitments or unreleased locks. MSTRx issuer-level Token-2022 powers are outside project control and must be disclosed separately

## Restricted action mapping

| Vote outcome | Exact source and destination | Completion condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `ACCUMULATE` | No token movement | `finalize` sets `EXECUTED` after the delay and releases the exact frozen MSTRx to free reserve |
| `BUYBACK_HOLD` | Frozen MSTRx -> verified CAPITAL/MSTRx venue -> CAPITAL permanent-hold ATA | Entire commitment spent and all acquired CAPITAL held in one transaction |
| `BUYBACK_BURN` | Frozen MSTRx -> CAPITAL trader ATA -> `BurnChecked` through CAPITAL token program | Entire commitment spent and all acquired CAPITAL burned in the same transaction; verify mint supply and ATA deltas |
| `BUYBACK_LOCK` | Frozen MSTRx -> CAPITAL trader ATA -> proposal-specific CAPITAL lock ATA | Entire commitment spent and all acquired CAPITAL locked in the same transaction; term begins at recorded execution time |
| `LOCK_MSTRX` | Frozen MSTRx -> proposal-specific MSTRx lock ATA | Exact full amount transferred with Token-2022 transfer hook satisfied; maturity releases to free reserve, permanent term never releases |
| `MARKETING_SALE` | Frozen MSTRx -> a separately verified MSTRx/SOL sale venue -> SOL to immutable disclosed marketing pubkey | Entire commitment spent, actual SOL proceeds forwarded and no intermediate balance left in one transaction |

The owner selected a **sale into SOL**, not a direct MSTRx transfer. The Rust enum name is `MarketingSale` at ordinal 5. A passing SBF build and host tests do not make it executable. The fixed recipient is a SOL address and may not be overridden by the keeper

## Pump routes and the MSTRx hook

Pre-migration use Pump's `buy_exact_quote_in_v2(spendable_quote_in, min_tokens_out)`, not its `buy_v2` exact-base-output interface, for the entire frozen MSTRx amount. The official Pump IDL requires `user` to sign and the quote/base mint and token-program accounts to be passed. The gated program source constructs the instruction from the verified IDL and invokes it with the PDA signer; a keeper may only submit an output minimum **at least as strict as the immutable minimum stored in the winning option**, never arbitrary program accounts or a reduced spend. Pump fees are included in the frozen quote amount. The repository's installed `@pump-fun/pump-sdk@2.0.0` has a `buy_v2` builder but no `buy_exact_quote_in_v2` builder: offchain callers must use a reviewed IDL-pinned builder or a verified SDK upgrade

After graduation select only the canonical PumpSwap pool proven for this CAPITAL and MSTRx pair, and use PumpSwap `buy_exact_quote_in(spendable_quote_in, min_base_amount_out)` if available for the deployed pool version. Its effective quote reserves are the raw quote-vault balance **plus** `Pool::virtual_quote_reserves`; quoting solely from the raw ATA is wrong. Verify pool version and any new fee accounts at execution time. The repository's installed `@pump-fun/pump-swap-sdk@1.20.0` `buyQuoteInput` wrapper produces an exact-base-output `buy`, not the IDL's exact-quote-in instruction. The `user` signer and payer constraints, Token-2022 MSTRx transfer-hook extra accounts, and maximum CPI/compute depth are compatibility gates, not assumed working from the IDL alone. If a full buy cannot fit the available curve inventory or transaction limits, execution fails and holders can vote again over the unchanged commitment

One spending decision executes in one Solana transaction: read pre-balances, validate the exact proposal/action/current Pump or PumpSwap phase and approved accounts, move the entire frozen MSTRx amount into the separate trader ATA, invoke the verified venue with that PDA signer, reload the token accounts, require that no frozen MSTRx remains unspent, and immediately burn or escrow all acquired CAPITAL. Mark the proposal executed and release its active pointer only after the exact balance changes and destination are verified. Later free reserve deposits must remain untouched. Any swap, hook, price, full-fill, burn, lock or account-validation failure rolls back the transaction and leaves the complete commitment locked. Retrying the same decision is safe only while it remains active and unexecuted; after it succeeds, replay must fail. Migration before execution changes the verified venue, not the frozen amount

The same successful transaction must initialize a proposal-scoped, program-owned buyback execution receipt only **after** the full trade and final custody/burn checks. It must bind `schema_version`, proposal and config keys, winning action, exact Pump/PumpSwap program, CAPITAL mint, reserve vault, full MSTRx input, the immutable voted minimum, actual CAPITAL acquired, hold/lock destination or burn result, execution time and PDA bump. Its address must derive from the proposal key so an unrelated receipt cannot attest execution. The frontend may display status `EXECUTED` for `BUYBACK_*` only when the canonical receipt exists and all its immutable fields match the finalized proposal, with `actual_capital_raw >= min_output_raw`. An event or status byte alone is not execution evidence. `MARKETING_SALE` has a separate gated atomic receipt binding actual SOL forwarded to the fixed marketing recipient. These source paths and receipt parsers are not deployed or canary-verified; preserve the frontend's fail-closed status check until they are

A reserve-funded buy is itself a CAPITAL/MSTRx trade and may generate a new creator-fee receipt. Treat that later receipt as a separate new 60/40 ingress event, not as a refund or a reduction of the current proposal's exact spend

The undeployed `Proposal` account schema is v3, with an immutable per-option `min_output_raw` (raw CAPITAL for buybacks, lamports for marketing sale). The source has no partial-execution path, but it does contain gated swap instructions; the SBF build passed, but there is no onchain execution proof. `GOVERNANCE_PROPOSALS_RELEASED` remains `false`

The installed official Pump IDLs define `buy_exact_quote_in_v2` with **27 ordered accounts** and `buy_exact_quote_in` on PumpSwap with **23 ordered accounts**. Both mark their `user` account writable and signer. The exact-quote arguments are `spendable_quote_in` and a nonzero minimum base output; PumpSwap also has an `OptionBool` volume-tracking argument. The gated program source constructs those two exact instruction discriminators from reviewed IDL constants, with no keeper-supplied CPI bytes, arbitrary account vector or alternate router. For each phase, it must derive and validate the canonical curve/pool, base and quote mints and token programs, all user/venue ATAs, creator and fee PDA identities, fee program/config, global/volume accounts, signer privileges, and venue-owned state. Fee-recipient choices must be checked against current signed/owned venue config, not merely against a string supplied by the executor. The curve/PumpSwap phase switch must be inferred from verified live state at execution, not from a keeper flag. Version drift or a missing account should fail closed

The one-transaction executor can keep the current exact full commitment model. It must set `Config::committed_reserve_raw_mstrx` to zero only after the complete action succeeds atomically. On failure, the full amount remains committed and immediately eligible for a fresh holder ballot once the proposal has reached `executable_at`. The account decoders, launch panel and publisher must agree on the active, superseded and executed statuses. An event alone is not replay protection

The production instruction must bind the full frozen amount and enforce at least the holder-visible minimum output frozen in the winning option. The owner approved this immutable minimum as the sole price barrier, without a separate oracle or TWAP. The owner fixes a positive raw CAPITAL or SOL minimum before voting and the proposal displays it beside the exact frozen input. An offchain output prediction may inform that choice but is not an authorization condition: execution cannot lower the voted floor. If the market cannot deliver it or cannot spend the entire commitment, the transaction fails atomically and holders may immediately vote again over the unchanged amount. This absolute floor does **not** guarantee a fair market price or eliminate manipulation inside the allowed range. The exact pool depth, issuer hook status, PDA payer/rent behavior, compute use and CPI nesting must still be reproduced for both market phases. The owner declined a separate validator machine, so the disposable mainnet canary is the planned integration check, with real SOL costs and no production funds. No onchain proof exists yet

A short signed-action expiry and a positive voted minimum output for every exchange option are required. The v3 option stores that floor before voting. Before signing an execution transaction the control route verifies the immutable proposal, current venue identity and required accounts through two finalized RPC views; it may simulate the complete transaction, including Token-2022 hook metas, without requiring an unproven offchain exact-output quote. These checks do not eliminate sandwich or manipulated-spot-price risk; the disclosed fixed minimum is the only onchain price barrier selected for this version. If the entire frozen amount cannot execute safely in one transaction, the decision remains unexecuted and holders can vote again

## Unresolved release decisions and tests

1. Test the owner-selected `ACCUMULATE` finalization/release in the disposable canary, and prove a completed vote cannot be finalized twice or executed as another action
2. Select and verify the exact MSTRx-to-SOL venue and atomic SOL forwarding route for `MARKETING_SALE`; the recipient and every temporary account must be pinned, and the sale must have onchain output/price limits
3. Revoke the governance program's upgrade authority **after** review and verification of the final released binary and before any proposal, then prove the ProgramData constraint rejects an upgradeable instance. Canary-test immediate holder re-vote eligibility at the previous ballot's `executable_at`, immediate start and chosen duration of each replacement ballot, the initial ballot's 24-hour delay, old executor invalidation, repeated failed re-votes and explicit holder `ACCUMULATE` release; an owner emergency override would contradict the agreed freeze
4. Test the immutable voted minimum output against actual pool depth, including a complete rollback when the market delivers less. Prove the entire frozen amount can execute atomically in both market phases; if it cannot, leave the commitment intact for a new holder ballot
5. Verify the real CAPITAL mint/token program, exact Pump curve and canonical PumpSwap pool, MSTRx mint extensions and authorities, all transfer-hook metas, trade fees, PDA-as-`user` compatibility, account-rent behavior and compute units for both phases
6. Test free withdrawal during active and committed votes, failed execution retaining the full lock, new fee deposits staying free, duplicate/replayed execution rejection, partial curve fill rolling back, migration before execution, pausing, invalid pool/recipient/mint/program, permanent hold and burn irreversibility, lock maturity, and reserve/holder vault isolation
7. Do not ask the owner to create the disposable Pump token until the custody/executor code is reviewed, built for SBF, and deployed to the isolated test configuration with its release gates and exact program ID verified. A real mint, curve and migrated pool cannot be verified before that token exists: the disposable canary must prove the actual full ballot-to-execution flow in both phases before any main launch or public voting. Source-level swap executors and host tests alone do **not** meet the test-token gate
8. Reject the tempting shortcut of calling either Pump venue with the data-bearing `config` PDA as the `user`/payer or letting the keeper pass a generic swap CPI account vector. The route must use an exact reviewed account map, a system-owned trader PDA, the real Token-2022 mint extension state, and enforce the immutable voted minimum output onchain. The holder vote cannot become binding before those are tested for both curve and migrated pool
