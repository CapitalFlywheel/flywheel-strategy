# FLYWHEEL STRATEGY brand direction

## Locked identity

- Name: `FLYWHEEL STRATEGY`
- Token ticker: `CAPITAL`
- Public ticker notation: `$CAPITAL`

## Strategy visual-language reference

The official Strategy Q1 2025 media kit is the visual reference, not a source
of reusable project artwork. The reference system uses:

- orange `#FA660F` as its signature color;
- black, white, and very light gray supporting colors;
- oversized, tightly spaced sans-serif typography;
- strong rectangular layouts with large areas of negative space;
- black-on-orange, white-on-black, and orange-on-light color variants;
- an orange-to-light vertical fade in selected campaign materials;
- a compact standalone symbol with generous clear space around it.

FLYWHEEL STRATEGY must use an original flywheel/capital symbol. It must not
copy or modify Strategy's proprietary B symbol, official wordmark, or portrait
assets. Public surfaces must state that the project is independent and is not
affiliated with Strategy Inc.

Official reference:

- https://www.strategy.com/
- https://assets.contentstack.io/v3/assets/bltf8d808d9b8cebd37/blt79a08458ba54e953/67a2752d4bee0bd2ad185711/Media_Kit_-_Strategy.zip

## Required outputs

- X avatar that remains recognizable at small mobile sizes.
- X banner with a safe central composition after responsive cropping.
- Matching site system for desktop and mobile holder rewards and automatic-airdrop status
  governance, history, and wallet connection flows.

## Selected direction

Route H informed the original design system. The current approved X identity uses the faceted six-blade orange mark on black, with cinematic orange light, precise technical lines and generous negative space:

- black avatar with the faceted original flywheel mark, readable at small sizes;
- oversized condensed typography in white and orange on dark surfaces;
- restrained technical panels and fee-flow lines;
- no standalone `C` monogram or copied Strategy symbol;
- approved source files: `assets/brand/final/x-avatar-current-source.jpg` and `assets/brand/final/x-banner-current-source.png`.

The current X avatar and banner were approved on 2026-09-17

Production refinement:

- orange and black are the dominant colors;
- the primary avatar uses medium-weight flywheel geometry with clearly separated blades;
- the X banner contains one clean flywheel without faded duplicates or shadows;
- the primary orange banner field does not use a translucent background grid;
- the ticker is excluded from the X banner.
- `CAPITAL IN MOTION` is highlighted in orange on the black banner panel.

The earlier concept board is historical direction reference, not the current artwork. New social and site assets should follow the approved final sources and be produced at their intended output sizes

## Copy style

Do not place periods at the end of display-copy paragraphs on the website or
visual assets. This applies to headings, descriptions, labels, cards, buttons,
captions, and promotional lines. Other punctuation may be used when it is
needed for meaning.
