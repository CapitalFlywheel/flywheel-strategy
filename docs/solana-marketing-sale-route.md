# MSTRx-to-SOL marketing sale: pinned candidate and release boundary

Status, 24 September 2026: **read-only candidate verified; sale executor not released**. A winning `MARKETING_SALE` must consume only its frozen MSTRx commitment, sell it for SOL, and send the *actual* SOL proceeds to the fixed marketing pubkey in the same atomic transaction. The option is not a direct MSTRx transfer and neither a Raydium API quote nor an offchain keeper may choose another pool or recipient

The project cannot remove issuer powers

## Code added for fail-closed inspection

`services/solana/marketingSaleRoute.ts` pins the exact pool, program, config, vaults, observation and mints, checks the official account discriminators/layout, pool PDA and swap-enable bit, MSTRx extension state and token-vault identity, and requires two near-synchronous finalized RPC views with byte-identical accounts. It returns `executionPermitted: false` and `quoteStatus: NO_TICK_ARRAY_DEPTH_OR_ONCHAIN_PRICE_GUARD`. Its integer-only spot reference is **not** a swap quote, min-out, price oracle or authorization to send a transaction. It is deliberately not wired into a signer or the governance executor

The live opt-in check passed against both public RPCs on 24 September 2026: common finalized block slot `450050702`, byte-identical account views at slot `450050703`, `3,897,967,121,952` raw WSOL in the pool vault, `218,463,290,156` raw MSTRx and `9,305,242,878,817` active liquidity units. A one-MSTRx spot reference was about `1.401858671` SOL **before fees and price impact**. This is not a fill estimate or minimum output. Earlier probes exposed a shared finalized-consensus helper error on a skipped/unavailable `getBlock` slot; that helper was separately fixed to continue searching older slots while preserving two-RPC agreement, and the later opt-in check passed. Do not infer sale readiness from this inspection

## Required before release

1. Decode current tick arrays, bitmap extension, pool fee configuration and dynamic fee state from finalized onchain accounts. The [official SDK quote path](https://github.com/raydium-io/raydium-sdk-V2/blob/master/src/raydium/tradeV2/trade.ts) uses `PoolUtils.computeAmountOutFormat` with tick-array cache; a vault-balance ratio or initial spot is insufficient for a concentrated pool. The v3 ballot stores a positive minimum SOL output in lamports **before holders vote**, visibly paired with the exact frozen MSTRx input. A fresh quote for the entire frozen amount must use actual route depth and fees under two-RPC agreement; execution may raise the minimum but cannot lower the voted floor. The owner selected this absolute floor without a separate oracle/TWAP: it rejects worse output but does not prove a fair price within the allowed range
2. Build and independently review an onchain allowlisted Raydium `swap_v2` CPI for token1 MSTRx input, token0 WSOL output, exact pinned pool/config/vaults/observation, derived tick-array accounts and both token programs. The [official `swap_v2` accounts](https://github.com/raydium-io/raydium-clmm/blob/master/programs/amm/src/instructions/swap_v2.rs) require a signer payer and changing tick-array remaining accounts. The keeper cannot supply arbitrary remaining accounts or a meaningless `min_out`
3. Use a clean, data-empty, System-Program-owned trader PDA, not the data-bearing governance config PDA. In one transaction, transfer the entire frozen MSTRx amount to its Token-2022 account, swap it, measure actual WSOL delta, close/unwrap its WSOL account under the SPL Token Program, separate returned rent from trade proceeds, and transfer exactly those proceeds to the immutable marketing pubkey. Any unspent MSTRx, hook, account, slippage, unwrap or SOL-forward failure rolls back the complete sale and leaves the full commitment locked
4. Enforce the exact committed input and the positive minimum SOL output frozen in the holder-visible ballot onchain; a keeper may raise, but never lower, that floor. Use a fresh independently checked quote and full transaction simulation before submission, while recognising that these offchain checks are not an additional onchain price guarantee. Test quote staleness, manipulation within the voted range, partial fills, duplicate execution, insufficient liquidity, pool mutation, token pause/hook activation, wrong recipient, rent leakage and interrupted retries. Mark the decision executed only when the whole frozen amount is sold and SOL forwarded in the same transaction. If the route remains unavailable when the previous ballot reaches `executable_at`, a fresh holder re-vote may be created and begin immediately over the unchanged full commitment. It still runs for the selected voting duration and waits five minutes after closing before finalization
5. Simulate the complete CPI/Token-2022/WSOL close/SOL forward transaction in a realistic validator environment, audit the final immutable program, then remove its upgrade authority before any real proposal or governed reserve funding. Until then the public voting/custody release gate remains closed

At no point should the team use the pool's current API TVL, `referenceSpotLamports`, an unverified aggregator route or a broad admin emergency withdrawal as a substitute for these conditions
