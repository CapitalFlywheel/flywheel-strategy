# FLYWHEEL STRATEGY brand guide

## Identity

- Name: `FLYWHEEL STRATEGY`
- Ticker: `CAPITAL`
- Public ticker: `$CAPITAL`
- Brand line: `CAPITAL IN MOTION`
- Mechanism line: `FEES → MSTR → HOLDERS`

## Colors

- Primary orange: `#FA660F`
- Primary black: `#080808`
- Supporting warm white: `#F5F0E9`

Orange and black must dominate every main surface. Warm white is only a
supporting color for readability

## Typography

- Large headlines: heavy condensed sans serif
- Technical labels: neutral sans serif with wide letter spacing
- Website implementation target: Anton for headlines and Space Grotesk for
  interface and body text
- Keep headlines short and direct

## Mark

- The mark contains six separate clockwise angular blades
- Keep visible space between the blades
- Never add a shadow or faded duplicate behind the primary mark
- Never turn the mark into a standalone letter C
- Use the black mark on orange or light backgrounds
- Use the orange or warm-white mark on black backgrounds

## Text rules

- Do not use periods at the end of headings, descriptions, cards, captions,
  buttons, or promotional paragraphs
- Do not add decorative punctuation that does not help the meaning
- Keep the writing simple and factual

## X assets

- Primary avatar: `x-avatar-1200.png`
- Primary banner: `x-banner-1500x500.png`
- Current source avatar: `x-avatar-current-source.jpg`
- Current source banner: `x-banner-current-source.png`
- The current X visual direction was replaced and approved on 2026-09-17
- Use the faceted orange three-dimensional mark on a pure black field as the
  primary reference for all new X content
- Use cinematic orange lighting, deep blacks, restrained technical lines and
  generous negative space
- For mechanism visuals, preserve the readable sequence
  `FEES → MSTR → HOLDERS`
- Do not place `$CAPITAL` on the X banner
- `$CAPITAL` may be used in post templates and token announcements

## Independence

The project may reference the MSTR treasury strategy as inspiration but must
not copy Strategy Inc proprietary marks or imply official affiliation
